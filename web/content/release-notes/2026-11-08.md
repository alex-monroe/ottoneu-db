# Week of November 2–8, 2026

A much friendlier phone experience, a podcast soundboard guide, and fixes that keep the live score worm and projection data accurate.

## New features

### Mobile
- **Easier to use on a phone.** On /lineup, the starter dropdowns are no longer squeezed into a sliver on small screens, and a sticky bar at the bottom shows your live projected total with a quick Optimize button while you scroll. Text boxes and dropdowns across the site are now large enough that iPhones no longer zoom in when you tap them. On mobile, the header shows a visible "The SOFA" home link next to the menu button, with an expandable player search. (#820)

### Podcast
- **Soundboard guide on the power-rankings reveal.** At /podcast/power-rankings/reveal, each revealed team now has a small 4x4 soundboard diagram beside its name with that team's clip button highlighted. (#813)

## Fixes
- **Live score worm no longer drops Monday-night plays.** Plays from one of the two data feeds could be thrown out when the other feed's games came first, so Monday night's scores never reached the worm. Both feeds are now read correctly, and the affected team's total picked up the missing points. (#810)
- **Live score worm restarts itself mid-game.** If the live worm stopped partway through a slate of games, it used to stay down until the next kickoff. It is now restarted within about five minutes while games are still on. (#811)
- **Cleaner data behind the projections.** Missing team info on historical player stats had skewed team usage totals, and a stats update had created duplicate copies of about 700 players. Both are fixed, and the duplicates were merged. The projection model showed no meaningful change in accuracy, apart from a small improvement for kickers. (#814, #817)

## Internal changes
- New weekly NFL stats data (per-player game logs and team offensive volume, refreshed Tuesdays during the season), plus added stat columns and player IDs, as groundwork for a new stat-based projection model (#815, #816, #821).
- Experimental stat-based projection models were tested against the current model. None beat it, so projections are unchanged: `stat_a01_marcel` (baseline), `stat_a02_age` and `stat_a03_role_prior` are kept as baselines, and `stat_a04_role_prior_rates` was rejected (#824, #825, #826, #827).
- Planning docs for the stat-projection redesign, including a stability study and the role of market inputs such as ADP (#815, #818, #823).
- Developer tooling: fixed the Antigravity pre-tool guard hook path (#819).
