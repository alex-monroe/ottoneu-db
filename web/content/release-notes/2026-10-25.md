# Week of October 18–25, 2026

Smarter playoff odds, keeper values that look ahead, a score worm for matchups and auction posts in their own Discord channel.

## New features

### Standings and playoffs
- **Week-by-week playoff odds.** Each remaining game is now projected as a head-to-head matchup between the lineups both teams can actually field that week, with players on an NFL bye benched. Unplayed games on team pages show a projected score and win chance. (#781)

### Trades and keepers
- **Keepers valued as a look at next season.** Keeper value on /trades and the trade builder at /trades/builder now treats a keeper as an option on next season's value, including how likely the player is to be worth keeping, instead of a single read of this year's pace minus salary. (#783)

### Matchups
- **Score worm.** A chart of both teams' running totals across the week shows the lead swing back and forth. It sits under the scoreboard on each game page (/scoreboard/[gameId]) and on /matchup once the game has kicked off, with a line noting the lead changes and each side's biggest lead. Scores are now collected every 5 minutes during NFL game windows, up from every 30. (#787)

### Discord
- **Free-agent auction posts in #auctions.** The league bot now posts when an FA auction opens (position, injury, minimum bid, when it closes and links to bid) and when it closes (winner, price, every bid and who started it). These go to the #auctions channel and run every 10 minutes all year. (#789, #791)
- **Daily adds-and-cuts digest paused.** The daily Discord post of yesterday's adds and cuts was too noisy next to the trade posts, so it has stopped. Trade and pick'em posts are unchanged. (#779)

### Site
- **Release notes at /release-notes.** One card per week, newest first, linked from the site footer. (#778)

## Internal changes
- A weekly job now opens a pull request each Monday with draft release notes for the past week's merged changes (#780).
- Analysis of the league's trade history to estimate how managers swap keeper value for this-season value. The data supports only a league-wide rate, so nothing on the site uses it yet (#784).
- The auction bot moved off its separate Railway hosting onto a scheduled GitHub job (#789).
