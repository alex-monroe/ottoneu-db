# Week of October 26–November 1, 2026

A much richer score worm, with live play-by-play scoring, win odds and projection cones, plus more accurate scoring and a fix for tied auctions.

## New features

### Score worm
- **Play-by-play worms for finished weeks.** Once a week is over, the score worm on game pages (/scoreboard) and /matchup is redrawn from every play instead of a few straight lines between scrapes. The tooltip names who scored (e.g. "G. Pickens +1.80"). (#795)
- **Minutes played view.** A new Clock | Minutes played toggle plots each team's score against the share of its own lineup's game minutes played, so a team with Sunday starters isn't made to look ahead of one with Monday-night starters. (#798)
- **Win % view.** A third view plots the left team's win probability play by play, from the pre-game odds to the final whistle. The tooltip shows both teams' chances, and the footnote names the pre-game favorite and the winner's low point. (#799)
- **80% "hurricane" cone.** Hover any moment on the Clock or Minutes played view to shade each team's likely range of scores from that point on. It starts narrow at the hovered point and widens toward each team's likely final score. The tooltip adds the likely final range for both teams. (#800, #801)
- **Live worm during games.** While NFL games are on, the worm is drawn play by play every minute, with all three views and the cone. Win % shows the current odds instead of a winner. (#806)
- **Live headline score.** While games are on, the main score on /scoreboard, game pages and /matchup is the live running total, with Ottoneu's official score shown small beneath it. Ottoneu's score takes over once the matchup is final or all starters are done. /scoreboard also refreshes itself every minute during live games. (#807)

### Discord
- **Trade posts move to a trade channel.** Trade posts are set up to go to their own channel, and will start once that channel is connected. (#804)
- **Some posts paused.** The pick'em, power-rankings and vote-reminder posts are no longer sent automatically. (#804)

## Fixes
- **More accurate points.** Fumbles lost (−2), two-point conversions (+2 for the runner or receiver, +1 for the passer) and missed field goals and extra points (−1) now count in every points calculation on the site. Player projections were checked and no model changed. (#797)
- **Live projection in overtime.** A game in Ottoneu's fifth quarter is now treated as overtime, so a team with one starter left no longer gets a phantom extra half-game of projected points. In one case this had inflated a live projection by about 19 points. (#808)
- **Tied auctions go to the right team.** A free-agent auction with tied top bids now goes to the tied team lowest in the standings, so the 🏆 in the Discord "Auction won" post lands on the correct winner. The post also notes when the top bid was tied. A post that already went out wrongly was not corrected. (#802)

## Internal changes
- A new `define-done` agent skill that has the agent write down how it will check its work before starting a task (#793).
