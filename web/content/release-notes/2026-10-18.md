# Week of October 11–18, 2026

Trade tools, playoff odds, a live free-agent board and a league feed in Discord.

## New features

### Trades
- **Trade history at /trades.** See every league trade, including cap loans and trades still pending in their 48-hour veto window. Each trade is valued three ways (this season's value, keeper value, and cap room), and sell-offs get a "did it pay off?" verdict based on the seller's next season. (#758)
- **Trade builder at /trades/builder.** Pick players and cap loans from any two rosters and see how each side's starting lineup, rest-of-season value, keeper value, cap room and roster spots change before you propose it. The link carries the trade, so you can share it. (#771)
- **Replay past trades.** Every trade on /trades now links to the trade builder with both rosters rewound to just before the deal, so you can judge it on this season's production. (#772)

### Standings and playoffs
- **Playoff odds and a buy/sell signal.** Standings on /scoreboard, the homepage and team pages now show each team's playoff odds, #1-seed odds and expected wins, simulated from weekly projections. Until the trade deadline, each team is also tagged as a buyer, seller or hold. (#773)

### Free agents
- **Open auctions and waivers on /free-agents.** A live board of open FA auctions and waiver claims, with countdowns, a suggested max bid for each auction, and a claim-or-pass call for each waiver player. Refreshed every three hours during the season. (#774)
- **Smarter upgrade flags.** A free agent is now flagged only if they would actually crack your optimal starting lineup (or fill a bye week), instead of being compared with your weakest bench stash. A new "Upgrades only" filter shows just those players. (#769)

### Discord
- **League feed in Discord.** The league bot now posts trades (when pending and when processed, with salaries and cap loans and a link to replay the trade), a daily digest of adds and cuts, and pick'em results and Thursday reminders. (#775, #777)

### Sharing and recaps
- **Link previews.** Links to /power-rankings and individual game pages now unfurl in chat apps with the top five teams or the game's score. (#764)
- **Weekly podcast recap** now includes the week's trades, adds and cuts. (#755)

## Fixes
- **Fresher data on the site.** Pages now refresh as soon as a data scrape finishes, instead of waiting out each page's cache timer. (#762)
- **Player-card scrape survives rate limiting.** The scrape now paces its requests and backs off when Ottoneu throttles it, instead of aborting. (#753)
- **Arbitration scrape no longer fails out of season.** It now checks the league calendar before running, which stops a false failure alert that fired every six hours. (#770)

## Internal changes
